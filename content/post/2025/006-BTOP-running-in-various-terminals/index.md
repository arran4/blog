Short comparison of how `btop` renders in a few terminals on Gen2.

Available terminals include:

- Windows Terminal
- Kitty
- Alacritty
- GNOME Terminal
- xfce4-terminal
- tmux
- WezTerm
- Konsole
- Terminator
- LXTerminal

`btop` generally behaves the same, though small tweaks like fonts or `TERM` may help.
